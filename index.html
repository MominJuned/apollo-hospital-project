Help & Support

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our multispeciality hospital services, treatments, doctors, appointments, and insurance tie-ups.

Why is Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad considered one of the best multispeciality hospitals in Ahmedabad? +
Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad is widely regarded as one of the leading multispeciality hospitals in Ahmedabad due to its strong clinical expertise, advanced medical infrastructure, and integrated care delivery. With state-of-the-art robotic surgical systems, 24x7 emergency response, JCI and NABH international accreditations, and over 200 renowned super-specialists across Cardiology, Oncology, Neurosciences, Gastroenterology, and Organ Transplants, patients receive gold-standard medical care.
Which areas in Ahmedabad does Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad serve? +
Apollo Hospitals operates multiple strategic hubs across Ahmedabad, including our flagship tertiary hospital at Bhat (Gandhinagar-Ahmedabad highway), Apollo Speciality Hospital at Ellisbridge (near Parimal Garden), and Apollo CVHF Heart Institute. We serve patients across Vastrapur, Bodakdev, Satellite, Bopal, Navrangpura, Maninagar, Chandkheda, Gandhinagar, and the entire Gujarat region.
What makes Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad a top-rated and patient-preferred hospital? +
Our patient-centric philosophy, high surgical success rates (over 99.6% in cardiac care), minimally invasive robotic precision, transparent billing, 24x7 emergency and trauma services, and dedicated international patient care desk make us the preferred hospital for families across Western India.
How can I book an appointment? +
You can book an appointment seamlessly through our website by filling the Request a Callback form, calling our domestic appointment desk at 1860-500-1066, using the Apollo 24|7 portal, or by walking in to any of our hospital registration counters in Ahmedabad.
What multispeciality services are offered at Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad? +
We offer over 35 specialized clinical departments including Cardiac Sciences, Cancer Care (Surgical, Medical, Radiation Oncology), Neurosciences, Orthopaedics & Robotic Joint Replacement, Gastroenterology & Hepatology, Nephrology & Urology, Multi-Organ Transplants, Pulmonology, Paediatrics, Obstetrics & Gynaecology, Critical Care, and Emergency Medicine.
Does Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad accept insurance? +
Yes, Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad works with all major insurance providers and TPAs (Third-Party Administrators) in India. Our dedicated TPA Help Desk assists with cashless hospitalization, pre-authorizations, documentation, and seamless claim processing.
How transparent are treatment costs at Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad? +
We maintain complete transparency in our billing. Prior to procedures, our financial counsellors provide comprehensive cost estimates covering surgery, room rent, pharmacy, and follow-up consults, ensuring patients and families face no hidden surprises.
Does Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad offer 24x7 emergency services? +
Yes, our Emergency and Trauma Centre is operational 24 hours a day, 365 days a year. Equipped with advanced cardiac life support (ACLS) ambulances, emergency doctors, rapid-response stroke & heart attack protocols (STEMI), and direct access to catheterization labs and operating rooms. Dial 1066 for immediate ambulance dispatch.
Does Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad offer preventive health checkups? +
Yes, we offer Apollo ProHealth — personalized, AI-powered health evaluations designed to detect potential diseases before symptoms appear. Programs range from essential wellness screenings to executive comprehensive master health checks with complimentary specialist reviews.
What safety and quality protocols does Apollo Hospitals Ahmedabad follow? +
Apollo follows stringent international clinical quality parameters, including the Apollo Clinical Access Program, TASCC infection control guidelines, strict surgical safety checklists, and regular clinical audits aligned with Joint Commission International (JCI) benchmarks.
